Building the best EV charging experience in America.

Role Senior Product Designer
Team 1 Product Designer
3 Engineers
My contribution User research, Experience design, Interaction design, Design system

Gravity operates America's fastest EV charging network. The company is backed by Google Ventures and is expanding rapidly across the country.

Audience

Research
