Redesign project

Fitness Tracker App Revamp

Elevating the user experience for a leading health application through intuitive design and data visualization.

Role UX/UI Designer
Industry Health & Fitness
Duration 3 Months

Stage 1: Usability Audit

We started by analyzing the existing application to identify pain points. Users reported that the navigation was confusing and that the data visualization was hard to interpret. We conducted user interviews and heuristic evaluations to gather qualitative data.

Stage 2: Design Strategy

Based on our findings, we developed a strategy focused on simplification and personalization. We decided to introduce a dashboard that adapts to the user's goals and a bottom navigation bar for easy access to core features.

Stage 3: Prototype Development

We moved through several stages of prototyping:

  • Wireframing: Establishing the layout and flow.
  • High-Fidelity: Applying the visual language.
  • User Interface Design: Refining the details and interactions.

Outcomes

The redesign resulted in a 40% increase in user engagement and a significantly higher satisfaction score. The new data visualization components were particularly well-received.
